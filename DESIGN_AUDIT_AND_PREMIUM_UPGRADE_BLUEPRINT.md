### 4.2 Typography & Editorial Rhythm
- **Primary Serif Display (Headings & Statements):**
  - Font: `Georgia`, `Baskerville`, `Canela`, or `Playfair Display`, serif.
  - Weight: 400 with delicate italic styling for semantic highlights.
  - Letter-spacing: `-0.035em` for dramatic, elegant headlines.
- **Primary Sans Interface (Body, Meta, Navigation, Controls):**
  - Font: `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `SF Pro Display`, `Inter`, system-ui, sans-serif.
  - Weights: `400` (Body), `500` (Sub-body), `600` (Buttons/Interactive), `700` (Badges/Labels).
  - Letter-spacing: `+0.08em` uppercase for eyebrow labels, `-0.015em` for reading body.
- **Scale:**
  - Hero `h1`: `clamp(3.4rem, 7vw, 6.8rem)` / `line-height: 1.02`
  - Section `h2`: `clamp(2.2rem, 4.5vw, 3.8rem)` / `line-height: 1.1`
  - Card `h3`: `1.35rem` to `1.55rem` / `line-height: 1.25`
  - Lede Paragraph: `1.15rem` / `line-height: 1.65`
  - Eyebrow / Label: `0.72rem` / `letter-spacing: 0.18em` / `text-transform: uppercase`

## 5. Structural & Placement Upgrade Specifications (Section by Section)

- **Current Issue:** Fixed 76px bar with plain bottom border and basic link list.
- **Upgrade Blueprint:**
