## Editorial and rights position

- The collection is a source-weighted research index, **not an official national membership roster**.
- The site must distinguish a public professional record from an affiliation claim. Most affiliation claims remain marked **source review** until a durable first-party, court, or person-controlled source is linked.
- Portraits, downloaded social-media images, opaque research citations, and legal/controversy narratives are deliberately excluded from the public-facing collection pending editorial and rights review.
- A TGP Party-list designation is not, by itself, proof of personal Tau Gamma Phi membership.
