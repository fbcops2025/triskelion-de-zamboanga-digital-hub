# Founder portrait rights notes

## Decision

No portrait is selected. No image file is included in this staging package. Search-result availability, an unwatermarked copy, a social-media post, or a public URL is not permission to reuse a person's portrait.

## Exact blocker

The requested four-image hero needs four real, identity-confirmed portraits. This pass found no portrait with both (a) reliable identity provenance and (b) documented website reuse permission. Using public web images would risk a false likeness, copyright infringement, or implied endorsement.

## Approval packet needed

For each founder, supply an original file or original source URL plus:

- photographer/owner or estate/rightsholder;
- written permission or license covering this website, hero crop, responsive derivatives, and archival retention;
- required credit line and source URL;
- confirmation that the person depicted is the named founder;
- any privacy, publicity, trademark, or takedown conditions.

Until then, status remains `client approval required`; do not copy into `src/assets`, `public`, `dist`, or the hero.

## Rejected approach

No AI-generated faces, generic historical portraits, celebrity lookalikes, cropped people from random chapter posts, or stock “fraternity founder” imagery were used.
