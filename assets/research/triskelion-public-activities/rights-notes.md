# Rights notes

## Decision

No image is rights-approved for website reuse in this run. No candidate was downloaded, copied, optimized, or placed in a public/static folder.

## Why courtesy is insufficient

The requested HTML caption format—`Photo courtesy of [named chapter/org]` plus an original-post link—can be prepared only after the named chapter/org or the actual photographer/copyright owner gives permission. A courtesy line is attribution, not a license. A public Facebook/YouTube upload is not automatically reusable, and a news article’s embedded photo remains subject to the publisher/photographer’s rights.

## Permission needed for each candidate

Obtain written permission from the actual photographer/copyright owner, or from an organization that confirms it owns/controls the image and is authorized to grant website reuse. The release should identify:

- exact image/post URL or attached file;
- photographer/creator and owner;
- permission for the Tau Gamma Phi public website, including downloading, local staging, optimization, and public display;
- required credit wording and any restrictions/expiration;
- confirmation that identifiable donors/minors are appropriately consented, or provide a wide shot with no sensitive medical details.

- Tau Gamma Phi – Zamboanga City Council South District Facebook page/post: **not approved / access-limited**. Public-channel identity is plausible, but no image-level owner or permission evidence was accessible without login.
- Triskelion de STA. MARIA 1968 Facebook post: **not approved / search lead only**. Snippet is not rights evidence.

## No legal-clearance claim

This is a documented research decision, not legal advice or a claim that any source is public domain.
