# Tau Gamma Phi national materials and membership-strength research

**Prepared:** 2026-10-02 (Asia/Manila)  
**Purpose:** Records and publication planning for the Triskelion Legacy & Impact platform.  
**Research rule:** A public image or page is a discovery lead. It is not automatically an official asset, proof of organizational authority, or permission to republish.

## Executive finding

There is no publicly maintained, current, authoritative nationwide membership census that could support an exact 2026 Philippines-only total.

The strongest public number located in this pass is a **2017 attributed estimate of about 800,000 members**, including community chapters known as the Triskelion Youth Movement. The Senate-hosted research material also repeats a separate claim of **more than one million registered members worldwide, including Sigmas**. Those figures are not directly comparable: they have different dates, scopes, definitions, and source chains. They should not be converted into a current Philippines-only count.

For public records, the defensible wording is:

> Tau Gamma Phi has been described in public sources as having a membership numbering in the hundreds of thousands, with some sources reporting more than one million worldwide when the wider Triskelion family is included. A current, independently verified Philippines-only census was not located.

For planning dashboards, use **“membership: unverified; public estimates only”** rather than a headline number. A future count should define whether it measures active members, good-standing members, lifetime members, fraternity-only members, or the wider fraternity/sorority/youth/alumni/diaspora family.

## What the public constitutional materials say about organization

The publicly available Global Constitution Q&A describes a structure beginning with active-member chapters, with chapters forming Areas and Areas forming Clusters. It says school chapters remain and sit with community chapters under an Area Council. It lists 12 clusters, including NCR North, NCR South, Philippine regional groupings, and international groupings. This is a reported structure in that document, not proof that every council currently uses it or that all bodies are under one uncontested authority.

The National Executive Council constitution copy available through Scribd describes the official Tau Gamma Phi seal as a large circle with a smaller circle inside; “TAU GAMMA PHI” on the upper portion; “FORTIS VOLUNTAS FRATERNITAS” on the lower portion; “19” and “68” at the sides; the three-legged symbol and Greek letters Tau, Gamma, and Phi inside; and black and gold as the seal colors. Because the accessible copy is hosted on Scribd, preserve it as a public constitutional reference lead and obtain an authorized copy before treating it as the final controlling document.

The same accessible constitution copy states that the National Executive Council has a role in final approval of recognition for newly created chapters or councils. The project should therefore keep a distinction between:

- a **chapter/council publicly claiming an identity**;
- a **chapter/council listed by a public directory or website**; and
- a **chapter/council confirmed as recognized by the relevant authorized body**.

### Public-record boundaries

The directory intentionally does not publish:

- private member names, phone numbers, email addresses, or registration details;
- officer names taken from social posts without a current official roster;
- claims that every named body is currently active;
- claims that every named body belongs to one uncontested national structure;
- exact member totals inferred from chapter names or social-media reach; or
- downloaded logos/photos as approved website assets without permission.

Each publicly named body should receive a follow-up record with `last_checked`, `current_activity`, `recognizing_body`, `source_owner`, and `confirmation_contact` before being promoted from public lead to verified organization.

### Recommended estimate language

**Public copy:** “Public sources describe the wider Triskelion family as numbering in the hundreds of thousands, with some claims exceeding one million worldwide. No current, independently verified Philippines-only census was located.”

**Internal planning:** “Use a broad planning label of **hundreds of thousands nationwide, unverified**, and retain the 800,000 figure only as a dated attributed estimate. Do not label 800,000 as active members, current members, or Philippines-only members.”

**Research target:** Ask the relevant authorized national or global records office for a dated export containing unique member count, active/good-standing count, member classification, council/area, and deduplication method. Without those fields, a large number is not auditable.

## Recommended records schema

For each chapter, council, insignia, or image, record:

- `record_id`
- `body_type`: school chapter, community chapter, city/municipal council, provincial council, regional/area council, national body, alumni, sorority, youth, diaspora
- `claimed_name`
- `location`
- `parent_or_area_claimed`
- `source_url`
- `source_kind`: official site, council site, government/registry, news, social page, directory, user-supplied
- `authority_status`: supplied, self-claimed, publicly listed, council-confirmed, national-confirmed, unknown
- `asset_url` and local filename, if any
- `creator_or_owner`
- `license_or_permission`
- `permission_contact/date`
- `publication_status`: discovery, internal comparison, approval needed, approved, rejected
- `last_checked`
- `notes_on_conflicting_governance`

## How to download safely

1. Prefer the original page or direct original-file link. Do not use a search-result thumbnail as the master file.
2. Save the original file without editing it first. Record URL, access date, filename, dimensions, and hash.
3. Save a screenshot of the source page showing the title, uploader/owner, and license or permission statement.
4. Keep a separate optimized derivative for website use. Never overwrite the original.
5. For a chapter/council seal or photograph, request written permission even if the page is public. Ask for the exact credit line and whether web download, optimization, and re-hosting are permitted.
6. For the Wikimedia seal, retain the Commons page and license rationale with the downloaded file. Treat trademark/organizational-identity questions separately from copyright.
7. For the user-supplied seal, preserve the original local file and the provenance note. The user’s authorization is the current project basis for use.

## Publication gates

An image may be marked **approved for publication** only when the record contains:

- a stable original URL or supplied-file provenance;
- clear body/chapter/council identity;
- owner or creator information;
- license or written permission appropriate to the requested use;
- credit wording and restrictions;
- no unresolved privacy issue involving identifiable people, minors, donors, or sensitive medical details; and
- human approval for the specific website placement.

## Decision

The project can safely build a **research/reference library** now, containing source links, supplied assets, public image leads, council directories, and approval states. It should not yet publish a nationwide member count or a gallery of council/chapter insignia as if all items were centrally official. The next highest-value evidence request is a dated, authorized membership and recognized-chapter register from the responsible records authority.
