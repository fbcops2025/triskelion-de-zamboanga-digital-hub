### Content model

The system uses two layers:

1. **Research layer** - evidence, uncertainty, sources, conflicts, and records that still need confirmation.
2. **Publication layer** - polished, reader-first website copy built only from material safe enough to publish.

Do not copy research notes directly into a public page. Translate verified or appropriately attributed information into natural website content, then place source notes at the bottom or behind a source link.
