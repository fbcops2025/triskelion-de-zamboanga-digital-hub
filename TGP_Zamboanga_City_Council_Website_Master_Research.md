### Evidence vocabulary

- **VERIFIED:** direct primary source or primary source plus independent corroboration, with a retained URL/document.
- **PRIMARY-SOURCE CLAIM:** stated by a council-controlled channel, but original archival proof or an independent source is absent.
- **SECONDARY-SOURCE CLAIM:** credible reporting or a documented third-party source; do not present as a council statement.
- **UNVERIFIED:** a lead, recollection, graphic, search snippet, or incomplete record. Not publishable as fact.
- **CONFLICTING:** more than one account exists; retain each account and seek a primary record.

## 2. Executive website brief

The public website should be a **source-aware record of local history and continuing civic activity**, not a replacement for private council communication. It should help members, families, students, community partners, researchers, and the public find approved information about history, chapters, service, public events, and verified contact channels.

Publicly appropriate: approved council history, chapter directory entries, leadership roles/terms with consent, service records, public notices, event recaps, public documents, citations, correction requests, and rights-cleared media.

Never expose: member rosters, initiation or ritual material, private addresses/numbers, donor/patient/beneficiary personal data, confidential complaints, minors without documented consent, security details, or unverified allegations.

The site supports historical preservation, public information, chapter continuity, service documentation, transparency about source quality, searchable archiving, community engagement, and discoverability. It should not claim national authorization, official endorsement, or a complete membership directory unless the relevant authority provides that approval.

## 11. Brand and visual identity observation

### A. Observed existing identity

The owner-supplied council page and research dossier indicate Tau Gamma Phi/Triskelion terminology and a service-oriented public message. They do **not** establish a complete formal brand manual, official color palette, typography standard, logo ownership chain, or permission to reuse social graphics.

### B. Proposed website direction

Use a calm civic-archive aesthetic: charcoal/near-black, warm archival ivory, restrained gold, and a safety-red reserved for corrections/alerts. Proposed colors: `#11161C`, `#FFFDF7`, `#C8A64B`, `#E6D7B0`, `#A63A3A`. Use dignified serif display typography with highly readable sans-serif body text, documentary captions, source chips, and a visible verification label. Avoid gaming, nightclub, campaign, or aggressive-template language.
