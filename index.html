Safe Membership and Accountability

Sa fraternity at sa wider member community, walang puwang ang hazing. Dapat malinaw ang safety, dignity, at respeto sa bawat isa.

Editorial safety standard · not an official quotation Membership should never require abuse as proof of belonging.

Under the Anti-Hazing Act of 2018 (RA 11053), all forms of hazing in school-based and community-based organizations are strictly prohibited. The Triskelion Legacy & Impact Platform enshrines active accountability:

  • Zero tolerance: A strict ban on physical, psychological, and degrading acts during recruitment and initiation.
  • Safe activity standards: Activities should follow applicable law and any confirmed council safeguards.
  • Member rights: Every candidate and member has a right to bodily integrity and dignity.
  • Local responsibility: Questions or safety concerns belong with the responsible chapter or council through its verified public channel; this national archive does not receive complaints or safety reports.
No national complaint intake Chapter or council responsibility

This page keeps the anti-hazing and safe-membership standard for education. It does not collect complaints, incident details, safety reports, or personal contact information.

Contact the responsible chapter or council through its verified public channel. No chapter or council contact is listed here unless it has been verified for publication.

Help preserve the history

Some of the most important records may still be at home

Former officers, members, chapters, councils, families, and researchers can contribute materials for historical review. A submission is reviewed before anything is published.

Every record can add to the larger story

An old folder, photo album, newspaper clipping, chapter file, certificate, or personal memory may hold a piece of history that deserves to be preserved. If a public record needs correction, share the source so it can be reviewed with care.

Archival submission standards:
  • Provide the original source if known.
  • Include approximate year, chapter, and city.
  • Each submission receives a tracking ID and is routed for historian review.
  • Corrections preserve prior versions in the audit log for historical transparency.
